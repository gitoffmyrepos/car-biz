  <project_overview>
    This platform supports a business that buys body-damaged vehicles from auctions (e.g., Copart/IAAI),
    repairs them, and leases/rents them to customers with a weekly payment plan.

    Weekly payment model example:
    - $150/week
    - Vehicle is serviced by admin at end of month
    - Payments are made externally via Zelle/CashApp; no payment processor integration
    - Payments are recorded manually after customer uploads proof (screenshot)

    Customer experience:
    - Marketing/SEO website to learn about service
    - Contact/inquiry form -> autoresponse email via Resend
    - Signup + secure authentication via Keycloak
    - Customer profile + verification
    - Upload driver insurance documentation (highly sensitive)
    - Request a vehicle, see availability/status
    - Upload payment proof (screenshot)
    - Get notified: "verification takes 48 hours"
    - After admin verifies, customer notified: "payment confirmed"

    Admin experience:
    - Admin portal to manage customers, vehicles, trackers, payments, due dates
    - Track each customer’s weekly due date and payment history
    - Manual payment verification workflow
    - Vehicle tracker inventory and assignment to vehicles
    - Delinquency workflow (late fees + escalation)
    - Recovery/tow workflow (compliance-gated) and permanent ban on final recovery action
  </project_overview>
